# Quantum Computing Challenges
### Challenge 1: Single-Qubit Measurement Predictions
**File:** `challenge_1_solution.ipynb`

- Create a single-qubit quantum circuit.
- Predict measurement outcomes before executing the circuit.
- Compare theoretical predictions with actual measurement results.

### Challenge 2: Biased Quantum Coin
**File:** `challenge_2.ipynb`

- Simulate a quantum coin flip.
- Explore how quantum gates influence measurement probabilities.
- Investigate how a qubit can produce biased outcomes.

### Challenge 3: Changing Bell-State Correlations
**File:** `challenge_3_solution.ipynb`

- Explore entangled two-qubit Bell states.
- Investigate correlations between measurement outcomes.
- Observe how quantum operations affect those correlations.

### Challenge 4: Switching Between Matching and Opposite Outcomes
**File:** `challenge_4_solution.ipynb`

- Experiment with two-qubit measurement outcomes.
- Investigate matching and opposite measurement results.
- Understand how quantum gates affect correlations between qubits


---

**Purpose:** To develop practical knowledge of quantum circuits and quantum measurement through hands-on experimentation.
